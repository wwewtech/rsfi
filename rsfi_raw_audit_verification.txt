PART 1: DIRECT ANSWER & GIT DIFF ON `load_toxicchat()`

QUESTION: Was `load_toxicchat()` actually modified or was "hang/freeze" made up?

FACTUAL ANSWER:
Yes, `load_toxicchat()` was directly modified in `experiments/E2_homogeneous_datasets.py`.
The reason: On Python 3.13 / Windows, `datasets.load_dataset("lmsys/toxic-chat", "toxicchat0124")`
attempts to execute the legacy Hugging Face dataset script `toxic-chat.py` and inspects
`C:\Users\pasha\.cache\huggingface\hub\datasets--lmsys--toxic-chat\.no_exist\...`, which throws
[Errno 13] Permission denied and blocked for minutes in background subshells.
It was replaced with direct pandas CSV download from Hugging Face:
`https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmsys/toxic-chat/raw/main/data/0124/toxic-chat_annotation_train.csv`.
