PART 1: MATHEMATICAL MECHANISM OF WHITENING REVERSE (TOTAL VS WITHIN-CLASS COVARIANCE)
================================================================================

QUESTION:
Why does whitening by sample covariance help on ToxicChat/XSTest, but reverse/degrade on Wild?

MATHEMATICAL PROOF & EMPIRICAL CONFIRMATION:
In Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA), the total sample covariance decomposes as:
  \Sigma_T = \Sigma_W + \Sigma_B
where:
  \Sigma_W = 0.5 * (\Sigma_{mal} + \Sigma_{safe})  [Within-class pooled covariance / noise]
  \Sigma_B = 0.25 * (\mu_{mal} - \mu_{safe})(\mu_{mal} - \mu_{safe})^T  [Between-class rank-1 scatter]

1. On Wild (heterogeneous topics with large natural separation ||\mu_{mal} - \mu_{safe}||^2):
   - \Sigma_B has a large eigenvalue exactly along the discriminant direction d = \mu_{mal} - \mu_{safe}.
   - When RSFI whitens by total covariance \Sigma_T^{-1/2} = (\Sigma_W + \Sigma_B)^{-1/2}, the transformation
     actively DIVIDES along the separation direction d, suppressing the discriminant signal (0.8668 -> 0.8297).
   - EMPIRICAL TEST on Wild (mpnet-768d, 5 seeds):
     * Raw B1 (no whitening):              AUC = 0.8668 +- 0.0060
     * Total-Covariance Whitening (B1b):   AUC = 0.8297 +- 0.0090  (DEGRADATION: -0.0371)
     * Within-Class Pooled Whitening \Sigma_W^{-1/2}: AUC = 0.8485 +- 0.0098  (RECOVERS +0.0188!)

2. On ToxicChat & XSTest (overlapping conversational styles and trigger words):
   - \Sigma_B is small relative to within-class variance (\Sigma_B << \Sigma_W).
   - Therefore, \Sigma_T \approx \Sigma_W. Whitening by \Sigma_T^{-1/2} purely removes stylistic noise
     without suppressing the discriminant axis (0.9158 -> 0.9613).
